Features

Everything you need to sing along

Right on time

Lines light up with the music, with the next one in view. Tap any line to jump there.

Two languages at once

Romanization for Korean, Japanese and Chinese, YouTube's translated captions, or Apple's on-device translation (macOS 26).

Pick your spot

Cover, Cover + Line, Lyrics or Mini, in Glass, Tinted or Clear. The current line can also sit in the menu bar or the notch.

Lyrics that find themselves

YouTube Music's own lyrics first, then LRCLIB, timed lyrics always preferred. Wrong version? Pick another, or import your own .lrc.

Light on your Mac

About 1% CPU while playing and next to nothing when idle. The music never plays twice.

Feels native

Liquid Glass on macOS 26, artwork-tinted colors, resize from any edge, click-through mode, VoiceOver and Korean UI.

Privacy

Playback stays in your browser

Subline doesn't play or record anything. It only receives what it needs to draw the lines.

Your browsermusic keeps playing here
→ title & artwork → playback clock → lyric lines
Sublineshows the lines on top
  • No account, analytics or tracking. Your cookies and YouTube session are never read.
  • Lyrics lookups send the song's title, artist, album and length to lrclib.net, and results are cached on your Mac.
  • The extension sends data only to the Subline app on your Mac. It never runs in private windows.
Install

Up and running in a minute

Download the app

Unzip, move Subline to Applications and open it. A ♪ appears in the menu bar and a setup window opens.

YouTube? Add the extension

Chrome, Arc, Brave, Edge or Vivaldi. Add it from the setup window, then reload your YouTube tabs.

Press play

The overlay appears on its own. Spotify and Apple Music need nothing else; macOS asks once if Subline may see what's playing.
